Essential

kova_site_language remembers language for up to 12 months; kova_cookie_consent_v2 remembers your choice for 180 days. Previous consent to local counters does not authorize Google Analytics.

Google Analytics 4

After consent on the public kovacompass.app domain (including www), we load Google tag G-2LE3GWYDZM. Google may store _ga and _ga_2LE3GWYDZM cookies for browser and session recognition. We configure 180-day expiry without automatic renewal on every visit; browsers may shorten this period.

What we measure

Page views, opening and playing the presentation, chapter selection, enlarged screenshots, opened FAQs and contact clicks. Our events contain only the event name, public page without URL parameters or fragments, and language. We do not send message contents, finances, account IDs or Workspace contents. Local previews do not run Google Analytics.

Recipient and basis

Publisher: Vadyslav Konohrai, Poland; contact: contact@kovacompass.app. Optional measurement relies on your consent. Google Analytics also processes technical browser, device, session and approximate location information. IP addresses are involved in communication; Google states GA4 does not store IP addresses. This does not make the data fully anonymous. Processing may take place outside the EEA. See Google’s privacy policy and GA4 data information.

Site language

The language choice also remains in localStorage until browser storage is cleared. KOVA account data is not stored here.

Change your choice

Open “Cookie settings” in the footer and reject analytics, or turn it off and save. Refusing does not limit the site. Withdrawal removes accessible GA cookies and reloads the page to stop the tag. It cannot recall data already sent. Contact us about data rights; you may also complain to the relevant data protection authority. Privacy documents remain readable without choosing consent.

Pre-publication status

Draft information updated 22 October 2026. Integration is website-only; the Offline V1 app remains analytics-free. Google Signals and advertising consent are disabled in code. Before publication the publisher must confirm processing terms, transfers, GA4 service retention (separate from cookie lifetime), disabled automatic enhanced measurement, and hosting configuration. Local tests do not verify these settings.
