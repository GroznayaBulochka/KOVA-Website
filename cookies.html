KOVA cookie policy

Small files. Clear choices.

This page explains which cookies KOVA uses, why they exist and how you can change your choice.

01

Essential consent cookie

kova_cookie_consent remembers whether you chose analytics cookies or only necessary cookies. It does not contain personal financial data.

02

Analytics cookies

If you opt in, anonymous usage cookies help us understand visits and improve the site. They are not used for advertising.

03

Your choice

Analytics cookies are optional and are never created before consent. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice at any time.

04

No advertising cookies

KOVA does not use cookies to serve targeted advertising or sell information about visitors.
