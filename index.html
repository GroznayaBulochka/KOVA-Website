Need to know

Questions, answered.

Is KOVA a bank?

No. KOVA is a private finance assistant that helps you understand, plan and improve your money habits. It never moves your money or acts as a bank.

Where is my data stored?

Your data stays encrypted on your device, with backups controlled by you. KOVA is designed to give you useful insights without selling your financial information.

Does KOVA work offline?

Yes. Core tracking, budgets and insights continue to work offline, so your finances stay available wherever life takes you.
