Privacy Policy

Simple, transparent information about how KOVA handles your data.

Last updated: September 15, 2026
Overview Data we collect How we use data Storage and security Cookies and analytics Your rights Contact

KOVA is a personal finance assistant designed to help you understand your money while keeping you in control of your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the KOVA website or app, why it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Data we collect

Depending on how you use KOVA, we may process:

  • Financial information you enter, such as expenses, budgets, goals, income and notes.
  • Account information, if you create an account or contact us, such as your name and email address.
  • Technical information, such as device type, app version, language and basic diagnostic information needed to keep KOVA working.
  • Support messages and any information you choose to include when asking for help.

KOVA does not need access to your bank account to provide its core tracking and planning features. We do not ask for bank passwords, payment card numbers or authentication codes.

2. How we use data

We use information to provide and improve KOVA, including to:

  • organize your financial information and calculate the insights you request;
  • save your preferences and keep your experience consistent across supported devices;
  • respond to support requests and communicate important product updates;
  • protect the service, diagnose errors and prevent misuse; and
  • develop clearer, more useful financial planning tools.

We do not sell your personal financial information or use it to serve third-party advertising.

3. Storage and security

KOVA is built around privacy-first principles. Where supported, core financial data is processed locally on your device and the app is designed to work offline. If you enable backups or sync features, the relevant information may be encrypted and stored with the service providers needed to deliver those features.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls. No digital service can guarantee absolute security, so please keep your device and account credentials protected.

4. Cookies and analytics

KOVA uses an essential cookie to remember whether you accepted or declined optional cookies. If you choose analytics cookies, KOVA may store an anonymous identifier, a page-view count and the last page visited so we can understand how the site is used and improve it.

Analytics cookies are optional and are not enabled until you give permission. They are not used for advertising, and we do not sell the information they contain. You can change your choice at any time through Cookie settings in the footer.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may share limited information with trusted providers who help us host, secure, analyze or support KOVA. They may only process information for the services they provide to us and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users, or to defend the security and rights of KOVA.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as it is needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce agreements. You can request deletion of information associated with your account by contacting us. Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, restrict certain processing, or object to processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children’s privacy

KOVA is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when KOVA changes or when legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the updated date shown above.

10. Contact

If you have a privacy question or request, email us at hello@kova.app.
