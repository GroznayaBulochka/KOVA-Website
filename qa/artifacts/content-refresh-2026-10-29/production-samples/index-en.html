What is KOVA?

Less money stress. More room for your plans.

KOVA is an Android app for everyday personal finances. Keep expenses, budgets and work hours together, so you can see what is left after bills and plan the month ahead.

For students, working people and anyone who wants to make more intentional decisions about their own money.

What you gain with KOVA

See where your money goes

Record everyday spending and find it by category. Understand which costs make the biggest difference to your month.

Know what is left in your budget

Set category limits and check progress as you spend. Plan the next purchase with a clearer view of what remains.

Plan around changing income

Pro · Record work hours and rates to estimate earnings. Bring your work schedule and monthly budget into one plan.

A few simple answers.

Before you start using KOVA.

Will KOVA be free?

KOVA Premium. Life Planner · Pro. Price History · Pro. Premium includes Work & Income, Financial Calendar, all reports, receipt scanning, Tax Center and Vault. Basic money tracking, Notes, encryption, backup, restore and data export remain free. Saved records remain accessible. Premium is required for new operations in this module. Backup and export remain free. Only an eligible offer shown by Google Play can start a trial, after you confirm the purchase there. 30 days free, then the price displayed by Google Play before purchase (Billed every month / Billed once a year). Automatically renews unless cancelled before the trial ends. Availability is determined by Google Play. Trial eligibility is managed by Google Play, not a local installation timer or financial backup. Reinstalling KOVA does not reset the trial history on the same Play account. This is not a guarantee of one trial per natural person using different accounts. Basic Free use and access to stored financial records do not require starting a trial.

Can I download KOVA yet?

Public downloads are not available yet. Follow KOVA on Instagram for launch news. This page will link to the confirmed Google Play listing when the release is available. No launch date has been announced.

Does the app work without internet?

Financial records and the Free base work locally. Optional Premium purchase, restore and access refresh use Google Play and need connectivity. Current Premium access may require a new connected check after up to 48 hours or a phone restart. This does not delete your records or block basic use and export. No financial cloud sync or bank connection is offered.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. KOVA does not ask for your bank passwords. Accounts in the app help organise the financial information you record.

How much data does each analysis need?

You can record expenses and budgets from the start. Basic reports need 5 transactions across at least 3 different days.

  • Variable spending estimate: at least 7 observation days and 2 expenses from the last 3 months. This is the threshold for one calculation component, not all of Premium.
  • Advanced reports: at least 60 days of history, 75 transactions, 20 days with transactions, and at least 1 budget or goal. All these conditions must be met.
  • Forecasts: at least 90 days of history, 100 transactions across 3 different months, and regular income or a recurring payment (recorded or detected). All these conditions must be met.

Buying Premium does not replace the required history. Records in the profile count, including correctly imported data; the installation date alone is not enough. Forecasts remain estimates.

Are forecasts and net pay amounts guaranteed?

No. They are estimates based on recorded data and configuration. They may differ from actual payments and do not replace employer documents or individual advice.

Can this website access my finances?

No. The website introduces the project and does not connect to the financial database on your phone. Optional website analytics depends on your consent.

How do I protect my data or change phones?

Create a password-protected manual export and store it safely outside your phone. Import requires confirmation and replaces the supported data of the selected profile. It is a partial copy, not a full app image: embedded note images are included, but external attachments and receipt photos need separate copies. The publisher cannot restore data from the cloud or recover your backup password.

Does receipt scanning work offline?

OCR uses a model bundled with the app. Take a photo or choose one from your gallery through the Android picker. Recognition can be wrong — check the amount, currency, and date before saving. Denying camera access does not block file selection. Pro: Saved records remain accessible. Premium is required for new operations in this module. Backup and export remain free.

Which devices and audience is KOVA for?

KOVA is being prepared for Android phones running Android 7.0 or later. KOVA’s interface supports 27 languages; compatibility and language review are ongoing. Intended for ages 13+, subject to store and local requirements. No iOS or watch release is offered.
