Project status

Your everyday finances. A release in progress.

KOVA: Money & Budget is coming to Android with a free base and optional Premium. Public downloads and sales are not available yet.

In preparation · Free + optional Premium

What the first release brings

Life Planner · Pro. Price History · Pro. Premium includes Work & Income, Financial Calendar, all reports, receipt scanning, Tax Center and Vault. Basic money tracking, Notes, encryption, backup, restore and data export remain free. Features appear as you use KOVA and add the data they need. This also applies to Pro: a subscription does not reveal data-dependent analyses before enough relevant data is available.

Where we are now

We are refining the app and checking compatibility, accessibility and subscription flows. Selected purchase and restore tests have run, but the complete public release is still being prepared.

Your finances stay local

KOVA stores financial records on your phone. Google Play handles optional subscription purchase, restore and access checks separately. No financial cloud sync or bank connection is offered.

How to follow progress

Follow @kova_money on Instagram for previews and launch news. Price and the confirmed store link will be shared before release. No launch date is announced.

KOVA Development is the project brand. The app is not publicly available yet; test results do not amount to public-sales approval.
