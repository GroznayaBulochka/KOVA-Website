KOVA security

Privacy is part of the product.

A clear view of the safeguards, choices and principles that keep KOVA useful without asking for more data than it needs.

01

Local by default

Core financial data is designed to stay on your device and work offline wherever possible.

02

Protected in transit

When information needs to move, KOVA uses encrypted connections and access controls appropriate to the service.

03

You stay in control

Backups and optional connections are choices you make. Permissions should be clear, limited and reversible.

04

Report a concern

If you find a security issue, contact hello@kova.app with the details and we will review it responsibly.
